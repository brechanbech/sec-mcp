I couldn't find WBD's own stated cumulative figure for restructuring costs since the merger. Adding up the yearly restructuring charges it reported gives about **$4.79 billion** for 2022 through 2024, but that's my own sum, not a number WBD published.

| Fiscal year | Restructuring charges (XBRL `RestructuringCharges`) |
|---|---|
| 2022 (merger closed April 2022) | $3,757 million |
| 2023 | $585 million |
| 2024 | $447 million |
| **Sum, 2022–2024** | **≈ $4,789 million** |

The FY2024 10-K's full text does mention restructuring costs incurred since the Merger. However, the XBRL concept for a cumulative total isn't in the data, and the search tool doesn't return the text of the matching passage. I searched for "$4.8 billion" and "$4.7 billion" next to "since the Merger" and neither matched, so I couldn't confirm the exact figure WBD discloses.

Two reasons the sum may not equal WBD's figure:
- The 2022 amount covers the full calendar year, so it may include some Discovery restructuring from before the merger closed in April 2022.
- The 2022 charges may also include content impairments, which WBD might or might not count in a "merger-related" total.

To get the exact figure, check the Restructuring note in the FY2024 10-K at the link below.
