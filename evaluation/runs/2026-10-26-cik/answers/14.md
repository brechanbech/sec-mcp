U.S. Steel's inventory turnover for FY2024 was about **6.55 times**, or roughly 56 days of inventory on hand.

**Calculation** (inventory turnover = cost of goods sold ÷ average inventory)

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Cost of goods sold, FY2024 | $14,060M |
| Inventory, 31 Dec 2023 | $2,128M |
| Inventory, 31 Dec 2024 | $2,168M |
| Average inventory: (2,128 + 2,168) ÷ 2 | $2,148M |
| **Inventory turnover: 14,060 ÷ 2,148** | **≈ 6.55x** |
| Days inventory outstanding: 365 ÷ 6.55 | ≈ 55.8 days |

- If you use year-end inventory instead of the average, turnover is 14,060 ÷ 2,168 ≈ 6.49x.
- For comparison, FY2023 turnover was about 7.0x: COGS of $15,803M ÷ average inventory of $2,243.5M, from $2,359M and $2,128M. So stock moved somewhat more slowly in 2024, mainly because COGS fell.
- U.S. Steel no longer has a current ticker in SEC's list, so I looked it up by its company ID number (CIK 1163302).
- All figures come from the XBRL data filed with the 10-K. The inventory figure is net of valuation and LIFO reserves.

**Sources**
- U.S. Steel Form 10-K for FY2024, filed 2025-01-31, accession 0001163302-25-000018 (FY2024 COGS; inventory at 31 Dec 2024 and 31 Dec 2023; FY2023 COGS)
- U.S. Steel Form 10-K for FY2023, filed 2024-02-02, accession 0001163302-24-000009 (inventory at 31 Dec 2022, for the FY2023 comparison)
