I can only partly answer this. The search tools here return which filings match a phrase, with their dates, form types and file names. They don't return the text of the filings. So I can't quote or summarize what US Steel actually wrote about how the Nippon Steel merger affects its operations. I took "Nippton" to mean Nippon Steel.

What the filing record shows, up to 16 May 2025:

- **Risk disclosures in its annual and quarterly reports.** The 2024 10-K (filed 31 Jan 2025) and the Q1 2025 10-Q (filed 2 May 2025) are the only reports that matched a search combining "Nippon Steel," "Merger," "business operations," "uncertainty," "employees" and "customers." That points to a risk discussion of how the pending deal could disrupt the business, but I couldn't read the wording. The 10-Q also matched a search for "Nippon Steel," "Committee on Foreign Investment" (the US national-security review body, CFIUS), "review" and "President Trump," so it appears to cover the deal's regulatory status.
- **Response to the blocking order and a lawsuit.**
  - On 3 Jan 2025 US Steel filed an 8-K with two exhibits, named "a99p1potusorder" and "a99p2nipponblockcondemn." Going by those names, they carry the presidential order blocking the deal and a statement condemning the block.
  - On 6 Jan 2025 a second 8-K had an exhibit named "ex99p1nipponlitigationb," which suggests the deal parties went to court. It also matched a search for "Cleveland-Cliffs" and "lawsuit," but I couldn't confirm who was sued or on what grounds.
- **Continued push for the deal after the new review.** An 8-K covering events of 6 April (filed 8 Apr 2025) matched the CFIUS / President Trump search. US Steel then filed further shareholder communications (DEFA14A) on 9 Apr and 22 Apr 2025.
- **Q1 2025 earnings.** The 1 May 2025 earnings presentation (8-K exhibit) mentions Nippon Steel, CFIUS and President Trump.
- **A $565 million figure.** "$565 million" appears alongside "Nippon Steel" in US Steel's March 2025 shareholder communications (DEFA14A filed 25–26 Mar 2025) and in SEC correspondence (filed 14 and 18 Mar 2025). I couldn't see the context, so I can't say what the amount refers to.
- **A proxy fight.** Several matching filings were not US Steel's own. They are proxy filings by a dissident shareholder campaign, with one file name pointing to Ancora (DFAN14A, PREC14A, PRRN14A, DEFC14A, January–April 2025). They argue against the company's position, and US Steel's DEFA14A filings answer them.

**Not found with these tools:** the actual wording of US Steel's merger discussion. That includes its stated effects on operations, capital spending, workforce and customers, the merger terms, the termination conditions, and the context of the $565 million. To get it, read the merger and risk-factor sections of the 10-K and 10-Q listed below.
