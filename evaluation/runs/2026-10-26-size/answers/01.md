I couldn't get Netflix's average revenue per paying membership (ARM) for 2019 to 2024 with these tools. The figure isn't in the structured XBRL data: searches for "average" and "member" across all of Netflix's data returned nothing. Full-text search shows the phrase "average monthly revenue per paying membership" appears in Netflix's 10-K filings for 2019 through 2024, but it only returns links to the documents, not the text, so I couldn't read the actual figures. I'm not going to estimate them.

I could get Netflix's total annual revenue, which ARM is calculated from:

| Fiscal year | Revenue (US$) |
|---|---|
| 2019 | 20,156,447,000 |
| 2020 | 24,996,056,000 |
| 2021 | 29,697,844,000 |
| 2022 | 31,615,550,000 |
| 2023 | 33,723,297,000 |
| 2024 | 39,000,966,000 |

Revenue grew about 93.5% from 2019 to 2024, or roughly 14% a year. Without the yearly count of paid memberships I can't tell how much of that came from more members and how much from higher revenue per member. The ARM figures are in each 10-K's "Results of Operations" section (Item 7), broken out by region, at the links below.
