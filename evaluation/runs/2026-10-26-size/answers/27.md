I couldn't get Netflix's total 2025 material cash requirements with these tools. The figure is in the "Material Cash Requirements" table of the FY2024 Form 10-K, filed 27 Jan 2025. But that table is in the MD&A section, which isn't machine-tagged, so the structured data doesn't include the total. Full-text search confirms the table is in that filing but only links to the document, not its contents.

The largest part of that total can be confirmed. Netflix tagged its content obligations falling due in the next 12 months:
- **Content obligations due within 12 months of 31 Dec 2024 (i.e., in 2025): $11,424,696,000 (about $11.4 billion)**
- For comparison, the same figure a year earlier (due in 2024) was $10,328,923,000.

The other lines in the table aren't in the tagged data: debt principal and interest due in 2025, operating lease payments and any other obligations. So I can't work out or confirm the full total. You can read it in the "Material Cash Requirements" table in the MD&A (Item 7) of the 10-K linked below.

**Sources**
- Netflix Form 10-K for FY2024, filed 2025-01-27, accession 0001065280-25-000044 (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1065280/000106528025000044/nflx-20241231.htm). The $11.42B figure comes from its structured data, and full-text search confirmed the filing has the material cash requirements section.
- Netflix Form 10-K for FY2023, filed 2024-01-26, accession 0001065280-24-000030 (prior-year comparison only).
