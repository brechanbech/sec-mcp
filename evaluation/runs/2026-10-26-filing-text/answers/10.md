These are Airbnb's (ABNB) common shares outstanding as of April 18, 2025, from the cover page of its latest 10-Q filed by 16 May 2025:

Class A: 431,602,213 shares
Class B: 185,610,542 shares
Class C: 0 shares
Class H: 9,200,000 shares

Adding the four classes gives 626,412,755 shares.

The balance sheet in the same filing (as of March 31, 2025) lists Class H as 9 million shares issued but zero outstanding. That conflicts with the cover page figure above, which I have reported as the filing states it.
