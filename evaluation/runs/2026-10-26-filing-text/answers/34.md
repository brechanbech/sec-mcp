Uber's largest adjustment in its FY2023 Adjusted EBITDA reconciliation was **Stock-based compensation expense, at about $1.94 billion** ($1,935 million), which was added back.

The 2023 reconciliation from the 10-K:

| Line item | FY2023 ($M) |
|---|---|
| Net income attributable to Uber Technologies, Inc. | 1,887 |
| **Stock-based compensation expense** | **+1,935** |
| Other (income) expense, net | (1,844) |
| Depreciation and amortization | +823 |
| Interest expense | +633 |
| Net income attributable to non-controlling interests | +269 |
| Provision for income taxes | +213 |
| Goodwill and asset impairments/loss on sale of assets, net | +84 |
| Restructuring and related charges | +51 |
| Income from equity method investments | (48) |
| Acquisition, financing and divestitures related expenses | +36 |
| Legal, tax, and regulatory reserve changes and settlements | +9 |
| Loss on lease arrangement, net | +4 |
| **Adjusted EBITDA** | **4,052** |

- The second-largest item was "Other (income) expense, net." It was a deduction of about $1.84 billion, so it was close in size but in the opposite direction.
- If you mean the standard EBITDA add-backs only (interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization), the largest was depreciation and amortization at about $0.82 billion.

**Sources**
- Uber Technologies, Inc. Form 10-K for FY2023, filed 2024-02-15, accession 0001543151-24-000012 (MD&A, "Adjusted EBITDA reconciliation" table)
