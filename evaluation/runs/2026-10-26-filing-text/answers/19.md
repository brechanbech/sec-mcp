**Oracle (ORCL) effective tax rate, fiscal year ended May 31, 2024**

**KPI: Effective tax rate**
- FY2024 (ended 5/31/2024): **10.9%**
- FY2023 (ended 5/31/2023): **6.8%**
- **Delta: +410bps** (10.9% − 6.8%)

**KPI: Income tax expense (supporting figure)**
- FY2024: **$1,274 million**
- FY2023: **$623 million**
- Change: +$651 million, about 104% higher

The rate went up because tax expense roughly doubled, which outpaced the growth in pre-tax income. I didn't pull pre-tax income for these years directly. Dividing tax expense by the rate puts it at about $11.7B for FY2024, but that's a derived estimate, not a reported figure. I also didn't get the filing's explanation of the rate change, such as discrete items or changes in the earnings mix by jurisdiction.

The tool also returned data from 10-Ks filed after 16 May 2025. I ignored those; they show the same FY2024 and FY2023 rates.

**Sources**
- Oracle Corporation Form 10-K for FY2024, filed 2024-06-20, accession 0000950170-24-075605 (FY2024 and FY2023 rate and tax expense)
- Oracle Corporation Form 10-K for FY2023, filed 2023-06-20, accession 0000950170-23-028914 (confirms the FY2023 rate of 6.8% and tax expense of $623M)
