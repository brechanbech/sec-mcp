**BROS gross profit in 2026 comes out at about $467 million** under your assumptions.

**FY2024 starting point** (10-K for FY2024):
- Revenue: $1,281.0M
- Cost of sales: $940.9M
- Gross profit: $340.1M, which is a gross margin of **26.55%**

**Assumptions applied:**
- Revenue grows 30% a year for two years: $1,281.0M × 1.30² (1.69) = **$2,164.9M** in 2026
- Gross margin falls by 500bps: 26.55% − 5.00% = **21.55%**

**2026 gross profit:** $2,164.9M × 21.55% ≈ **$466.6M, or about $467M**

Notes:
- Dutch Bros does not report a gross profit line. I calculated it as revenue minus the "cost of sales" figure it reports. That cost line includes most shop-level operating costs, which is why the margin is lower than you might expect.
- As instructed, I used only filings dated on or before 16 May 2025. Newer data in EDGAR, including the FY2025 10-K filed 2026-02-13, was left out.
