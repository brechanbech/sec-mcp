3D Systems' nine non-employee directors received **$2,263,113** in total compensation for 2023. That breaks down into $913,113 in cash fees and $1,350,000 in stock awards. The stock figure is the grant-date value of the annual equity award of 18,094 shares each, priced at $8.29 a share on May 16, 2023. I added up the per-director totals myself; the proxy doesn't print a grand total.
