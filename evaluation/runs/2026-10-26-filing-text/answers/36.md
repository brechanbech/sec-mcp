As of December 31, 2024, Warner Bros. Discovery had incurred **about $4.7 billion in total pre-tax restructuring charges** related to the April 2022 merger with the WarnerMedia business.

The FY2024 10-K (MD&A section) gives this context:
- **Original estimate:** When the restructuring framework was finalized in 2022, the company expected **$4.1–$5.3 billion** in pre-tax restructuring charges. The $4.7 billion incurred falls inside that range.
- **Cash portion:** Of the expected total, the company expected **$1.0–$1.5 billion** to be cash spending.
- **Scope:** The charges cover strategic content programming assessments, organization restructuring (including headcount cuts), facility consolidation and other contract termination costs.
- **Status:** The company said the merger-related restructuring program was "substantially completed" at the end of 2024, but its restructuring work is still ongoing.
