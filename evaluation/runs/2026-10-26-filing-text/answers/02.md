TJX **beat** its Q4 FY2025 pretax profit margin guidance, by **70 basis points over the top of the range**.

| | Pretax profit margin |
|---|---|
| Q4 FY25 guidance (given Nov 20, 2024) | 10.8% to 10.9% |
| Q4 FY25 actual (13 weeks ended Feb 1, 2025) | 11.6% |
| **Beat vs. high end (10.9%)** | **+70 bps** |
| Beat vs. midpoint (10.85%) | +75 bps |
| Beat vs. low end (10.8%) | +80 bps |

TJX's own release gives the same number: the Q4 margin "was above the high-end of its plan by 0.7 percentage points." The company said the beat came mainly from lower-than-expected inventory shrink expense and from spreading expenses over higher-than-planned sales. Some of that was offset by other items, but that part of the passage was cut off in what I read.

The Q4 margin was also 40 bps above last year's 11.2%. Last year's quarter had 14 weeks, so the cleaner comparison is the adjusted 10.9%, which takes out the 0.3-point extra-week benefit. Against that, the gain is 70 bps.

**Sources**
- TJX 8-K (Q3 FY25 earnings release, EX-99.1), filed 2024-11-20, accession 0000109198-24-000056: Q4 guidance of 10.8%–10.9%
- TJX 8-K (Q4 FY25 earnings release, EX-99.1), filed 2025-02-26, accession 0000109198-25-000006: actual Q4 margin of 11.6% and the 0.7-point beat over the high end
