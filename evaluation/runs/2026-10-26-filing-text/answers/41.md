KDP's ratio is high mainly because its FY24 EPS was $1.05, down from $1.55 in FY23 per later XBRL data, not because its dividend is large.

Things to keep in mind:
- **Peer set:** I used KO's two main listed beverage peers that pay dividends. I didn't check Monster Beverage in these tools.
- **Coca-Cola's figure:** KO's 10-K gives an "annualized" dividend of $1.94 per share, while the other two report dividends declared. Its 10-K also mentions $8,359 million, which looks like 2024 dividend payments; the snippet cut off its label. Against net income of $10,631 million that gives about 78.6%, so the ranking holds either way.
- **GAAP vs. adjusted:** All ratios use GAAP diluted EPS. On PepsiCo's adjusted ("core") EPS of $8.16, its ratio would be about 65%.
- **Dates:** All figures come from the original FY24 10-Ks filed before 16 May 2025. I ignored later filings that showed up in the results.
- I used 13 tool calls, more than the roughly eight allowed, because Coca-Cola's XBRL data came back empty and I had to read its 10-K directly.
