**Amazon (AMZN) is set to spend the most on capex in 2025.** One caveat: Meta is the only one of the three that put a dollar figure for 2025 in its SEC filings. Amazon's and Alphabet's filings say capex will rise but give no number, so the ranking is my inference from what they reported.

| | 2025 plan (from filings) | Q1 2025 capex | Last full year / trailing 12 months |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Amazon** | Expects cash capex "to increase in 2025," mainly for technology infrastructure (mostly AWS); no dollar figure | $24.3B (vs. $13.9B in Q1 2024) | 2024: $77.7B |
| **Alphabet** | Expects to increase technical-infrastructure investment compared with 2024; no dollar figure | $17.2B (vs. $12.0B in Q1 2024) | 12 months to Q1 2025: about $57.7B |
| **Meta** | **$64–72B** including finance-lease principal payments, up from the prior $60–65B outlook because of more AI data centers and pricier hardware | $13.69B | — |

Why Amazon comes out on top:
- **Amazon vs. Meta:** Amazon spent $77.7B in 2024 and says 2025 will be higher. That already beats the top of Meta's 2025 range ($72B). Amazon's Q1 spending was also about 1.8 times Meta's.
- **Amazon vs. Alphabet:** Amazon's 2024 total is about $20B above Alphabet's trailing 12 months, and its Q1 2025 spending was about 40% higher. Both say spending will rise, so Alphabet would need a much bigger jump to catch up.

**Likely order: Amazon > Alphabet > Meta.** Placing Alphabet ahead of Meta is less certain, because no Alphabet 2025 dollar target appears in these filings. Its trailing-12-month pace of about $57.7B and Q1 spending of $17.2B suggest it will end up above Meta's range.

**Not found with these tools:** Amazon's and Alphabet's specific 2025 capex numbers. Those companies seem to give them on earnings calls, and the transcripts aren't in EDGAR.
