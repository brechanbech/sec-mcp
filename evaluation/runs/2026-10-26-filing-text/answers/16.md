Airbnb's take rate went up each year from FY2022 to FY2024:

- **FY2022:** 13.3% (revenue $8,399M ÷ gross booking value $63,212M)
- **FY2023:** 13.5% (revenue $9,917M ÷ gross booking value $73,252M)
- **FY2024:** 13.6% (revenue $11,102M ÷ gross booking value $81,784M)

**Trend:** It rose by about 25 basis points in 2023 and then held at about 13.5–13.6% in 2024, as revenue grew at roughly the same 12% pace as gross booking value.

I worked out the rates from the figures in Airbnb's filings; Airbnb doesn't report them itself. Gross booking value is the total value of bookings, including host earnings, service fees, cleaning fees and taxes, so the take rate isn't a pure commission rate.

**Sources**
- Form 10-K for FY2024, filed 2025-02-13, accession 0001559720-25-000010: gross booking value for 2023–2024 and revenue for 2022–2024 (XBRL)
- Form 10-K for FY2023, filed 2024-02-16, accession 0001559720-24-000006: gross booking value for 2022 and revenue for 2022–2023 (XBRL)
- Form 10-K for FY2022, filed 2023-02-17, accession 0001559720-23-000003: revenue for 2022 (XBRL)
