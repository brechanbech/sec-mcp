In fiscal 2024, Airbnb added back **$1.407 billion** ($1,407 million) of stock-based compensation expense in its cash flow statement. This is the non-cash adjustment used to reconcile net income to cash from operating activities.

For comparison, the 2023 figure was $1.120 billion, so the 2024 amount was about 26% higher.

The value comes from the XBRL tag ShareBasedCompensation ("Share-based Payment Arrangement, Noncash Expense") in Airbnb's FY2024 10-K. A filing dated after 16 May 2025 also turned up in the results, so I didn't use it.

**Sources**
- Airbnb, Inc. Form 10-K for FY2024, filed 2025-02-13, accession 0001559720-25-000010 (XBRL financial data)
