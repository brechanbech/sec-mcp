Airbnb starts from GAAP net income and makes seven adjustments to get Adjusted EBITDA. This comes from its latest 10-Q as of 16 May 2025, for the quarter ended 31 March 2025. Adjusted EBITDA Margin is Adjusted EBITDA divided by revenue.

**The adjustments:**
1. **Provision for income taxes** is added back.
2. **Other expense, net** is added back.
3. **Interest income** is subtracted.
4. **Depreciation and amortization** is added back.
5. **Stock-based compensation expense** is added back.
6. **Acquisition-related impacts** are excluded. These are gains or losses from changes in the fair value of contingent consideration arrangements.
7. **Lodging taxes, host withholding taxes and transactional taxes, net** are excluded. This covers three things:
   - lodging taxes where Airbnb may share legal responsibility with hosts for collecting and remitting them,
   - withholding taxes on payments to hosts, plus any related settlements,
   - transactional taxes where it's unclear how they apply to Airbnb's platform.

Airbnb says it removes these items because they are non-cash, or because their amount and timing are unpredictable, aren't driven by core operations and make comparisons with past periods and competitors less meaningful.

**Reconciliation (US$ millions, three months ended 31 March):**

| | Q1 2024 | Q1 2025 |
|---|---|---|
| Net income | 264 | 154 |
| + Provision for income taxes | 29 | 19 |
| + Other expense, net | 10 | 38 |
| – Interest income | (202) | (173) |
| + Depreciation & amortization | 14 | 25 |
| + Stock-based compensation | 295 | 358 |
| + Acquisition-related impacts | 6 | — |
| + Lodging/host withholding/transactional taxes, net | 8 | (4) |
| **Adjusted EBITDA** | **424** | **417** |
| Revenue | 2,142 | 2,272 |
| Adjusted EBITDA Margin | 20% | 18% |

Stock-based compensation and interest income are by far the largest items. Airbnb's explanation for the lower Adjusted EBITDA in Q1 2025 is higher product development and sales and marketing spending, driven by employee pay and costs for upcoming product launches. Revenue growth from more nights and experiences booked partly offset this.
