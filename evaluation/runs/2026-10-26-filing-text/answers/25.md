As of June 30, 2024, about **44.7%** of Microsoft's full-time employees were based outside the United States.

**Figures from the filing:** Microsoft's FY2024 10-K, Part I, Item 1, says: "As of June 30, 2024, we employed approximately 228,000 people on a full-time basis, 126,000 in the U.S. and 102,000 internationally."

- Total full-time employees: about 228,000
- In the U.S.: about 126,000 (55.3%)
- Outside the U.S.: about 102,000

**Calculation:** 102,000 ÷ 228,000 = 0.4474, or about 44.7%.

Microsoft rounds these headcounts to the nearest thousand, so the percentage is approximate too.

**Sources**
- Microsoft Corp. Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended June 30, 2024, filed 2024-07-30, accession no. 0000950170-24-087843
