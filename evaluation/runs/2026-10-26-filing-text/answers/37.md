**Answer: a beat of about 29 bps.** Lyft's Q4'24 Adjusted EBITDA margin was about 2.64%, against a Q3'24 guidance midpoint of 2.35%.

- **Actual Q4'24:** Adjusted EBITDA of $112.8M divided by Gross Bookings of $4,278.9M is about 2.64%. Lyft rounds this to 2.6% in its release.
- **Guidance given with Q3'24 results (Nov 6, 2024):** an Adjusted EBITDA margin of about 2.3% to 2.4%, so the midpoint is 2.35%. The same guidance put Adjusted EBITDA at $100M–$105M and Gross Bookings at $4.28B–$4.35B.
- **Difference:** 2.64% minus 2.35% is a beat of about +29 bps. Using Lyft's rounded 2.6%, the beat is +25 bps.

If you instead build the midpoint from the dollar guidance ($102.5M ÷ $4.315B ≈ 2.38%), the beat is about +26 bps. Either way it's a beat: Adjusted EBITDA came in above the top of its guided range, while Gross Bookings landed at the bottom of theirs.
