**Answer: on normal March seasonality, TSMC lands slightly above the midpoint of its Q2 2025 guidance. That is a small beat of about US$0.13B (+0.4%) against the midpoint, still inside the range and about US$0.27B below the top.** The projection is about NT$940B, or about **US$28.9B**, against guidance of US$28.4–29.2B (midpoint US$28.8B). The answer depends heavily on which year you treat as "normal", so this isn't a strong call either way.

**Step 1 – Guidance (Q1'25 earnings release, 17 Apr 2025)**
- Q2'25 revenue of US$28.4–29.2B, using an exchange rate of 32.5 NT$ per US$.
- In NT$ that is about NT$923.0–949.0B, with a midpoint of NT$936.0B.

**Step 2 – How Q2 compares with March in past years (NT$ million)**
Q2 = January–June minus January–March.

| Year | March revenue | Jan–Mar | Jan–Jun | Q2 | Q2 ÷ March |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2022 | 171,967 | 491,076 | 1,025,217 | 534,141 | 3.106× |
| 2023 | 145,408 | 508,633 | 989,474 | 480,841 | 3.307× |
| 2024 | 195,211 | 592,644 | 1,266,154 | 673,510 | 3.450× |
| **3-year average** | | | | | **3.288×** |

**Step 3 – Project Q2 2025**
- March 2025 revenue was NT$285,957M.
- 285,957 × 3.288 ≈ NT$940.1B.
- At the guidance rate of 32.5, that is about **US$28.93B**.
- Against the US$28.8B midpoint: about +US$0.13B (+0.4%). Against the US$29.2B top: about −US$0.27B.

**How sensitive this is**
- Using 2022's ratio (3.106×) gives about US$27.3B, which would **miss** the low end by about US$1.1B.
- Using 2024's ratio (3.450×) gives about US$30.4B, which would **beat** the top by about US$1.2B.
- So the three-year spread (±US$1.5B) is about twice as wide as the guidance range (US$0.8B).

**Cross-check with April data, which tilts toward a beat**
- April 2025 was NT$349.6B, up 22.2% from March. That alone is about 37% of the March-based projection.
- In 2024, April (NT$236.0B) was 35.0% of Q2. Applying that share to 2025 gives about NT$997B, or about US$30.7B at 32.5. That would beat the top of guidance by about US$1.5B.
- This rests on one year of April data only. I didn't pull April figures for 2022 or 2023.

**Caveats**
- All the monthly figures are in NT$. If the actual exchange rate in Q2 differs from 32.5, the US$ result changes. A stronger NT$ would reduce reported US$ revenue relative to this projection.
- This is mechanical seasonality, not a forecast of demand.
