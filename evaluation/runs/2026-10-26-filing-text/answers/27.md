Netflix's material cash requirements for 2025 total about **$14.43 billion** ($14,426,266 thousand). The 10-K doesn't use the label "Total Projected Material Cash Requirements." It describes its "material cash requirements from known contractual and other obligations" in a table called "Contractual obligations." Its "Next 12 Months" column, measured from December 31, 2024, covers calendar 2025.

| Contractual obligations (in thousands) | Next 12 Months (2025) | Beyond 12 Months | Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| Content obligations | $11,424,696 | $11,824,235 | $23,248,931 |
| Debt (principal and interest) | 2,486,945 | 17,354,517 | 19,841,462 |
| Operating lease obligations | 514,625 | 2,246,495 | 2,761,120 |
| **Total** | **$14,426,266** | **$31,425,247** | **$45,851,513** |

- Content makes up about 79% of the 2025 total, at roughly $11.4 billion.
- Of the $23.2 billion in total content obligations, $17.0 billion is not on the balance sheet. The other $4.4 billion is in current content liabilities and $1.8 billion in non-current content liabilities.
- These figures are a snapshot as of year-end 2024 from the FY2024 10-K. They don't reflect anything Netflix committed to after that date.

**Sources**
- Netflix, Inc. Form 10-K for fiscal year 2024, filed 2025-01-27, accession 0001065280-25-000044 (MD&A, Liquidity and Capital Resources, contractual obligations table)
