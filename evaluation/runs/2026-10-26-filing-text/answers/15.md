KKR & Co. Inc. issued its **6.25% Series D Mandatory Convertible Preferred Stock** on March 7, 2025. The offering was enlarged twice, first from 30 million to 45 million shares at pricing, then by the underwriters' over-allotment option. Each share automatically converts into between 0.3312 and 0.4140 KKR common shares on or about March 1, 2028.

**Size of offering**
- The base offering was 45,000,000 shares, raised from the 30,000,000 shares first announced.
- Underwriters bought all 6,750,000 over-allotment shares, worth $337.5M of liquidation preference.
- Total issued: **51,750,000 shares, or $2,587,500,000 of aggregate liquidation preference.**
- Net proceeds were about **$2.53B** after underwriting discounts and estimated expenses.
- The lead underwriters were Morgan Stanley & Co. LLC and KKR Capital Markets LLC. The underwriting agreement was signed March 4, 2025.

**Key dates**
- Trade date was March 5, 2025.
- Settlement and closing was **March 7, 2025** (T+2). The Certificate of Designations was filed in Delaware and took effect the same day.

**Price**
- Each share was sold to the public at **$50.00**.
- The underwriting discount was $1.125 per share.

**Liquidation preference**
- **$50.00 per share**, plus accumulated and unpaid dividends (declared or not) up to the liquidation date.
- It is paid after creditors and any senior stock, and before common stock and other junior stock.

**Dividend rights**
- Dividends are **6.25% a year** on the $50 liquidation preference. They are cumulative and paid only if the board (or an authorised committee) declares them.
- KKR can pay them in cash, in common stock, or a mix, subject to limits.
- Payment dates are March 1, June 1, September 1 and December 1, from June 1, 2025 through March 1, 2028. Record dates are February 15, May 15, August 15 and November 15.
- The first dividend is expected to be about $0.7292 per share. Each one after that is expected to be $0.78125.
- If dividends are behind, KKR cannot pay dividends on common or other junior stock, or buy it back, with limited exceptions. Dividends on stock ranking equal (parity) with the Series D must then be shared pro rata with it.

**Conversion terms**
- **Mandatory conversion** is expected on March 1, 2028. The rate depends on the average volume-weighted price of KKR common stock over a 20-trading-day period beginning on the 21st scheduled trading day before that date:
  - Above the Threshold Appreciation Price of about $150.97: 0.3312 shares, the minimum rate.
  - Between the Initial Price of about $120.77 and $150.97: $50 divided by the market value.
  - Below $120.77: 0.4140 shares, the maximum rate.
- The rates are subject to anti-dilution adjustments. The Floor Price is $42.2705, about 35% of the Initial Price.
- **Early conversion by the holder:** any time before March 1, 2028, at the minimum rate of 0.3312. This does not apply during a fundamental-change conversion period.
- **Fundamental change:** holders may convert at a special rate set out in a table by date and stock price. They also receive a make-whole amount for future dividends, using a 4.77% discount rate, plus any accumulated dividends.

**Voting rights**
- Holders have no vote except where Delaware law or the certificate of incorporation requires one.
- If dividends go unpaid for six or more dividend periods, consecutive or not, the board grows by two seats. Holders then vote, together with other voting preferred stock, to elect those two directors.
- Holders of at least two-thirds of the Series D must approve three things:
  - creating or increasing any stock that ranks senior to it;
  - changes that hurt the Series D's rights;
  - certain mergers, share exchanges or reclassifications, unless the Series D survives on terms that are not materially less favourable.

**Purpose (use of proceeds)**
- To buy additional equity stakes in core private equity portfolio companies reported in KKR's Strategic Holdings segment, and for general corporate purposes.
- KKR intended to list the shares on the NYSE as "KKR PR D". Later filings show the ticker KKR-PD, which suggests the listing went ahead.
