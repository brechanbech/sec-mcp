Netflix's global average monthly revenue per paying membership (ARM, Netflix's term for revenue per paying user) went from **$10.82 in 2019 to $11.70 in 2024**. That is a rise of $0.88, or about 8% in total and roughly 1.6% a year. Over the same years, average paying memberships rose from about 153.0 million to 277.7 million, so most of the revenue growth came from more members rather than higher prices per member.

**Global ARM by year (reported in USD)**
| Year | ARM | Change |
|---|---|---|
| 2019 | $10.82 | – |
| 2020 | $10.91 | +1% |
| 2021 | $11.67 | +7% |
| 2022 | $11.76 | +1% |
| 2023 | $11.64 | −1% |
| 2024 | $11.70 | +1% (+4% in constant currency) |

ARM jumped in 2021, which Netflix put down to price changes and favourable exchange rates. From 2022 to 2024 it stayed between about $11.64 and $11.76.

**ARM by region, 2019 vs. 2024**
| Region | 2019 | 2024 | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| US & Canada (UCAN) | $12.57 | $17.20 | +$4.63 (+37%) |
| Europe, Middle East & Africa (EMEA) | $10.33 | $10.96 | +$0.63 (+6%) |
| Latin America (LATAM) | $8.21 | $8.24 | +$0.03 (flat) |
| Asia-Pacific (APAC) | $9.24 | $7.29 | −$1.95 (−21%) |

**What this means:**
- **UCAN drove the increase.** ARM there rose every year, from $12.57 to $17.20, which is about 6.5% a year compounded.
- **The regional mix held the global figure down.** Most new members came from lower-ARM regions: APAC average paying memberships went from 13.2 million to 50.5 million, and EMEA from 44.7 million to 94.2 million. Because those regions weigh more in the average, global ARM grew much less than UCAN ARM.
- **Exchange rates hid price increases in some regions.** In 2024, LATAM ARM fell 5% in US dollars but rose 21% in constant currency. APAC fell 5% in dollars and 3% in constant currency. Global ARM rose 1% in dollars and 4% in constant currency.

The total changes, compound annual growth rates and dollar differences are my own calculations from the reported figures.
