**Palantir's 3-year revenue CAGR is about 22.9%**, measured from fiscal 2021 to fiscal 2024. FY2024 is the latest full fiscal year reported in a filing made on or before 16 May 2025.

| Fiscal year (ends 31 Dec) | Revenue |
|---|---|
| FY2021 (start year) | $1,541,889,000 |
| FY2022 | $1,905,871,000 |
| FY2023 | $2,225,012,000 |
| FY2024 (end year) | $2,865,507,000 |

**Calculation:**
CAGR = (2,865,507,000 / 1,541,889,000)^(1/3) − 1 = (1.8584)^(1/3) − 1 ≈ **22.9%**

The revenue figures are Palantir's XBRL tag RevenueFromContractWithCustomerExcludingAssessedTax. The data also included a 10-K filed in February 2026 with FY2025 figures. I left it out because it is dated after 16 May 2025.
