AMD beat its implied non-GAAP gross profit guide in all four quarters. The average beat was about **2.8%**, or roughly $100 million a quarter.

Q2 2024 - $80 million (2.6 % BEAT)
Q3 2024 - $73 million (2.0 % BEAT)
Q4 2024 - $90 million (2.2 % BEAT)
Q1 2025 - $158 million (4.1 % BEAT)

**Average: 2.8 % BEAT** (simple average of the four quarterly percentages, which come to 2.65%, 2.02%, 2.22% and 4.12%)

How each guide was built (midpoint revenue guide × non-GAAP gross margin guide), compared with the reported non-GAAP gross profit:

| Quarter | Guide given | Implied gross profit | Actual non-GAAP gross profit | Beat |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Q2'24 | $5.7B × ~53% | $3,021M | $3,101M | +$80M |
| Q3'24 | $6.7B × ~53.5% | $3,585M | $3,657M | +$73M (precisely $72.5M) |
| Q4'24 | $7.5B × ~54% | $4,050M | $4,140M | +$90M |
| Q1'25 | $7.1B × ~54% | $3,834M | $3,992M | +$158M |

AMD gives its margin guidance as an approximate figure ("approximately X%"), so the implied gross profit numbers are estimates, not figures AMD published.
