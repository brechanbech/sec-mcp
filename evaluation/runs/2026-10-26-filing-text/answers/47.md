Lemonade beat the full-year 2024 guidance it gave with Q3 results (30 Oct 2024) on every revenue and profit measure. In force premium landed inside its range, near the top.

- **Earlier in the year:** In the Q3 letter, Lemonade raised its full-year guidance for gross earned premium and revenue and kept the rest unchanged. Its original February 2024 guidance was in force premium of $938–942M and an adjusted EBITDA loss of $160–155M. It beat both of those as well.
- **Other FY2024 figures in the Q4 letter:** Revenue grew from $429.8M in 2023. The net loss narrowed to $202.2M from $236.9M. Gross profit roughly doubled to $166.9M. The trailing-twelve-month gross loss ratio improved to 73% from 85%. Adjusted free cash flow was positive for the first full year, at $48M.
- **Q4 alone:** Q4 2024 guidance was in force premium of $940–944M, gross earned premium of $222–225M, revenue of $144–146M and an adjusted EBITDA loss of $29–25M. Actuals were $943.7M, $226.4M, $148.8M and a loss of $23.8M, so Q4 also came in above the top of each range.

Stock-based compensation and share count come from the FY2024 10-K's XBRL data. Everything else comes from the shareholder letters.
