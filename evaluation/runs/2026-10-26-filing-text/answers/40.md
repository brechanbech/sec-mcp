FND's same-store sales (the company calls them comparable store sales) **fell 0.8%** in Q4 of fiscal 2024, the quarter ended 26 December 2024, so there was no growth that quarter.

Other figures from the same release:
- Total net sales for the quarter were $1,107.4 million, up 5.7% from $1,048.1 million a year earlier. The increase came from new stores; the company opened 10 warehouse stores in the quarter and ended it with 251.
- Management said Q4 comparable store sales came in better than it had expected.
- For the full fiscal year 2024, comparable store sales fell 7.1%.
- Guidance for fiscal 2025 is comparable store sales of roughly flat to +3.0%.

A store enters the comparable base on the first day of the 13th full fiscal month after it opens.
