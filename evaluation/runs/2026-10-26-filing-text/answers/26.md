Allstate's FY2024 10-K (Note 14, Capital Structure) shows one junior subordinated issue outstanding at December 31, 2024: **$500 million of Series A 6.500% Fixed-to-Floating Rate Junior Subordinated Debentures**. The balance was the same $500 million a year earlier, at December 31, 2023.

**Maturity**
- The scheduled maturity date is May 15, 2057.
- The final maturity date is May 15, 2067. The debt table lists the issue as "6.500% Junior Subordinated Debentures, due 2067."

**Interest**
- Until May 15, 2037, interest is fixed at 6.500% and paid semi-annually.
- After that it floats and is paid quarterly. The original rate was three-month LIBOR plus 2.120%. LIBOR has been replaced by the three-month CME Term SOFR Reference Rate plus a spread adjustment of 0.26161%, so the effective rate is Term SOFR + 2.120% + 0.26161%.

**Redemption**
- On or after May 15, 2037, Allstate can redeem all or part of the issue at any time, at principal plus accrued and unpaid interest.
- Before May 15, 2037, it can redeem all or part only in certain circumstances. The price is principal plus accrued and unpaid interest or, if higher, a make-whole price.

**Interest deferral**
- Allstate can defer interest for one or more consecutive interest periods, up to 10 years. Deferred interest compounds at the rate in effect for each period.
- In certain circumstances, the deferral feature requires Allstate to issue common stock or certain other securities if it cannot otherwise raise enough money to pay the interest. It has reserved 75 million authorized but unissued common shares for this.
- While interest is deferred, Allstate generally cannot declare or pay dividends or distributions on its common or preferred stock. It also cannot redeem, buy, acquire or make liquidation payments on that stock. There are some limited exceptions.

**Replacement capital covenant (RCC)**
- Allstate entered into an RCC when it issued the debentures. The covenant protects holders of a designated "covered debt" series, currently the $800 million Floating Rate Subordinated Debentures due 2053. Holders of the junior subordinated debentures cannot enforce it.
- Under the RCC, Allstate will not repay, redeem or buy back the debentures on or before May 15, 2067 (or an earlier RCC termination date) unless it has received net cash proceeds in specified amounts from selling common stock or other qualifying securities. Certain limitations apply.
- The RCC does not apply if any of the following happens:
  1. S&P upgrades Allstate's issuer credit rating to A or above.
  2. Allstate redeems the debentures because of a tax event.
  3. A market disruption event after a redemption notice prevents Allstate from raising the proceeds the RCC requires.
  4. Allstate repurchases or redeems up to 10% of the outstanding principal in any one-year period, with no more than 25% in any ten-year period.
