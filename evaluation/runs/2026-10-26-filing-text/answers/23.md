Salesforce's debt, not counting the Sustainability Notes, had a face value of **$7.5 billion**. That is total principal of $8,500 million minus the $1,000 million 2028 Senior Sustainability Notes.

The most recent figures on file come from the fiscal 2025 10-K, filed 5 March 2025, and they are as of 31 January 2025. I found no filing showing the debt as of 10 March 2025 itself. The 10-K reports no borrowing under the revolving credit facility, and I didn't see any new debt issued or repaid between 31 January and 10 March. So the $7.5 billion should still apply on 10 March, but that is an inference.
