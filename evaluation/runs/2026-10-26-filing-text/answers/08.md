Micron beat the midpoint of its fiscal Q3 2024 GAAP gross margin guidance by about 140 basis points. The result came in just under the top of the range.

- **Guidance:** On 20 March 2024, Micron guided FQ3‑24 GAAP gross margin to 25.5% ± 1.5%. That's a range of 24.0% to 27.0%, with 25.5% as the midpoint.
- **Actual:** On 26 June 2024, Micron reported FQ3‑24 GAAP gross margin of 26.9%. In dollars that's $1,832M on revenue of $6,811M, or 26.90%.
- **Against the midpoint:** 26.9% − 25.5% = a beat of about +140 bps.
- **Against the high end:** 26.9% is about 10 bps below the 27.0% top of the range. So it beat the midpoint but did not clear the top of the range.

For reference, non-GAAP gross margin was 28.1% against guidance of 26.5% ± 1.5%. That beat the midpoint by about 160 bps and topped the 28.0% high end by about 10 bps.
