Production at McCalla is no longer just expected. Smucker says it already began, in the second quarter of fiscal 2025.

- **Original plan:** The FY2024 10-K (filed June 18, 2024) said construction began in 2022, "with production expected to begin in 2025." In the properties section it said "Production is expected to begin at the McCalla facility during 2025." Smucker uses fiscal years: FY2024 ended April 30, 2024, so fiscal 2025 runs from May 2024 to April 2025.
- **Latest update:** The Q3 FY2025 10-Q (filed Feb 27, 2025, for the quarter ending Jan 31, 2025) says "Construction of this facility began in 2022 and production began during the second quarter of 2025." With that fiscal calendar, the second quarter is roughly August to October 2024.

The site isn't only a distribution center. Smucker describes it as a $1.1 billion manufacturing facility and distribution center, announced in November 2021, built to make Uncrustables frozen sandwiches.

The ticker is SJM, not SJ.
