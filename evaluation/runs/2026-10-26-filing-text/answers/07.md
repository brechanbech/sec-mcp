TKO paid **about $3.25 billion plus a $50 million purchase price adjustment, so roughly $3.3 billion in total**, for the Endeavor businesses it acquired (PBR, IMG and On Location). The deal closed on 28 February 2025. TKO's Q1 2025 10-Q describes it this way:

- **Form of payment:** all in equity. TKO issued about 26.54 million common units of TKO OpCo to Endeavor OpCo and other subsidiaries of Endeavor Group Holdings (EGH), plus the same number of TKO Class B shares.
- **How the price was set:** the share count was based on the volume-weighted average price of TKO Class A stock over the 25 trading days ending 23 October 2024. That is before closing, so the ~$3.3 billion is the agreed price, not a value at closing.
- **No value at closing is disclosed:** EGH controlled both TKO (about 54% of voting interests before closing, about 61% after) and the acquired businesses. The deal was therefore accounted for as a merger of entities under common control. Their net assets were added at historical carrying amounts, and the financial statements were recast for prior periods. Purchase accounting was not used, so no value of the consideration on 28 February 2025 was reported. The equity issued simply increased the non-controlling interest in TKO OpCo, with a matching increase to additional paid-in capital.

A value of the consideration measured on the closing date is **not found with these tools**. The filings I searched do not give a fair value or closing-price value for the 26.54 million units. I did not read the 8-K exhibits filed 19 March and 8 May 2025 that a search returned, so they could not be ruled out.
