Cloudflare doesn't report what share of its customers came through channel partners. What it reports is the share of revenue. For fiscal 2024 (year ended December 31, 2024), **20% of revenue came from channel partners**, which was $337.4 million out of $1,669.6 million in total revenue.

Cloudflare's 10-K breaks down revenue by type of customer:

| Year | Channel partners | % of revenue | Direct customers | % of revenue | Total revenue |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2024 | $337,394K | **20%** | $1,332,232K | 80% | $1,669,626K |
| 2023 | $202,404K | 16% | $1,094,341K | 84% | $1,296,745K |
| 2022 | $122,522K | 13% | $852,719K | 87% | $975,241K |

The channel share has risen each year, from 13% in 2022 to 16% in 2023 and 20% in 2024. The 10-K describes these partners as managed service providers, resellers, distributors and global system integrators.

I couldn't find the share of *customers* (by count) that came through channel partners with these tools. The 10-K text I searched only gives the split by revenue.

**Sources**
- Cloudflare, Inc. Form 10-K for fiscal year 2024, filed 2025-02-20, accession 0001477333-25-000043 (revenue recognition policy and the note on revenue by type of customer)
