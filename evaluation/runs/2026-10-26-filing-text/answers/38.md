Delta's quarterly earnings releases contain a "Financial Guidance" table for the next quarter with **three metrics**: total revenue growth year over year, operating margin and earnings per share. The January release also gives full-year guidance for EPS growth, free cash flow and gross leverage. Delta says these guided figures are non-GAAP (adjusted).

**1. Next-quarter table (in every quarterly release)**

| Metric | Q1 2025 (given 10 Jan 2025) | Q1 2025, revised (10 Mar 2025) | Q2 2025 (given 9 Apr 2025) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Total revenue, year over year | Up 7% to 9% | Up 3% to 4% | Down 2% to up 2% |
| Operating margin | 6% to 8% | 4% to 5% | 11% to 14% |
| Earnings per share | $0.70 to $1.00 | $0.30 to $0.50 | $1.70 to $2.30 |

The revenue guide is "adjusted" revenue, which leaves out refinery sales to third parties. The March 2025 filing shows this: Q1 total revenue of $13.9–14.1B becomes $12.9–13.1B adjusted.

**2. Full-year table (January release, for 2025)**

| Metric | FY 2025 forecast | 3–5 year target |
|---|---|---|
| EPS growth, year over year | More than 10% (EPS above $7.35; the growth rate excludes the 45-cent impact of the 2024 CrowdStrike outage) | 10% on average |
| Free cash flow | More than $4B | $3–5B |
| Gross leverage (adjusted debt to EBITDAR) | 2x or less | 1x |

In April 2025 Delta said it was **not reaffirming** its full-year 2025 guidance because of uncertainty, and would update it later in the year.

**3. Other outlook statements (in the text, not the tables)**
- **Non-fuel unit cost (non-fuel CASM):** expected to grow by low single digits. The January release said this for full-year 2025; the April release said it for Q2 and the rest of the year.
- **Debt repayment:** "at least $3 billion" in 2025 (April release).
- The releases also describe revenue drivers such as premium, loyalty/American Express, international and corporate demand, and capacity growth. These are commentary rather than numeric guidance.

Delta can also revise guidance during a quarter, as it did with the 10 March 2025 8-K.
