Rocket Companies (RKT) agreed to buy Redfin for **$12.50 per share**, an **equity value of $1.75 billion** and a **total enterprise value of $2.4 billion**.

- **Price per share:** $12.50 per Redfin share. It is an all-stock deal, so there is no cash price. Each Redfin share converts into a fixed **0.7926 shares of Rocket Class A common stock**, plus cash in place of fractional shares. The $12.50 is the value of that exchange ratio when the deal was announced. Because the ratio is fixed, what holders actually receive moves with Rocket's share price.
- **Equity value:** $1.75 billion.
- **Enterprise value:** $2.4 billion.
- **Premium:** 63% over Redfin's volume-weighted average price for the 30 days ending 7 March 2025.

As of 16 May 2025 this was a signed merger agreement (dated 9 March 2025) that had not yet closed. Redfin filed its definitive merger proxy on 5 May 2025 for the shareholder vote. I did not check for a completion filing; any would come after that date.
