For the nine months ended 30 September 2024, loanDepot originated about **$17.31 billion** in loans. Purchases were about 70% of that and refinancings about 30%. These figures come from the 10‑Q filed on 12 November 2024.

| Loan purpose | 9M 2024 | Share | 9M 2023 | Share |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Purchase | $12,057,993K (~$12.06B) | ~69.7% | $12,403,166K (~$12.40B) | ~71.7% |
| Refinance | $5,250,321K (~$5.25B) | ~30.3% | $4,897,857K (~$4.90B) | ~28.3% |
| **Total** | **$17,308,314K (~$17.31B)** | 100% | $17,301,023K (~$17.30B) | 100% |

Year over year, total originations were roughly flat. Purchase volume fell about 2.8% and refinance volume rose about 7.2%.

For the third quarter of 2024 alone, purchases were $4.38B and refinancings $2.28B, for a total of $6.66B.

The filing's table doesn't show its column headers in the extracted text. I've assumed the usual 10‑Q order (Q3 2024, Q3 2023, 9M 2024, 9M 2023). The purchase and refinance figures add up to each column's total. I worked out the percentages and year-over-year changes myself; they are not stated in the filing.
