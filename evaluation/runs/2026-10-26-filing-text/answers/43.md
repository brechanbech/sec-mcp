In 2024, Airbnb's average nights per booking in Asia Pacific was **3.3**. That is the lowest of its four regions.

For context, the same 10-K gives these 2024 figures:
- North America: 4.1
- EMEA: 3.8
- Latin America: 3.7
- Asia Pacific: 3.3
- Whole company, excluding experiences: 3.8, down from 3.9 in 2023

Airbnb says it expects its blended global average nights per booking to keep fluctuating with changes in geographic mix and in how people travel.

**Sources**
- Airbnb, Inc. Form 10-K for fiscal year 2024, filed 2025-02-13, accession 0001559720-25-000010 (Management's Discussion and Analysis)
