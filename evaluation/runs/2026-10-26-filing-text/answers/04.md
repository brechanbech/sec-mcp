- **No new people:** the proxy says "All of our director nominees are currently serving on the Board," so these are all sitting directors up for re-election. The Board had eight seats at the time, and each nominee would serve until the next annual meeting.
- **Later 2024 change:** BBSI's 8-K of December 17, 2024 says Joseph S. Clabby was appointed to a newly created Vice Chairman role, effective December 13, 2024. That was a new role for a current director, not a new nomination.

I didn't find a 2024 press release announcing a separate new board nominee. A full-text search for "nominated to serve" in BBSI filings from 2024 to May 2025 returned nothing.
