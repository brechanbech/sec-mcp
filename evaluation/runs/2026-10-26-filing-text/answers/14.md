I calculate US Steel's FY2024 inventory turnover at about **6.55 times**. That means it cleared its average inventory roughly every 56 days.

I used the standard formula: cost of goods sold divided by average inventory. Both figures come from the FY2024 10-K's financial data (XBRL).

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| FY2024 cost of goods sold (calendar 2024) | $14,060 million |
| Inventory, net, 31 Dec 2023 | $2,128 million |
| Inventory, net, 31 Dec 2024 | $2,168 million |
| Average inventory | ($2,128M + $2,168M) / 2 = $2,148 million |
| **Inventory turnover** | $14,060M / $2,148M ≈ **6.55x** |
| Days inventory outstanding | 365 / 6.55 ≈ **55.8 days** |

- Using only year-end inventory gives $14,060M / $2,168M ≈ 6.49x.
- The year before, FY2023 turnover was about 7.0x ($15,803M divided by an average inventory of $2,243.5M). So inventory moved somewhat more slowly in 2024, mainly because cost of goods sold fell.
- The inventory figures are net of LIFO and valuation reserves. Turnover would come out differently on a non-LIFO basis.

Sources:
- United States Steel Corp (CIK 1163302), Form 10-K for FY2024, filed 2025-01-31, accession 0001163302-25-000018. This gives FY2024 cost of goods sold and inventory at 31 Dec 2023 and 31 Dec 2024.
- United States Steel Corp, Form 10-K for FY2023, filed 2024-02-02, accession 0001163302-24-000009. This gives the FY2023 comparison figures.
