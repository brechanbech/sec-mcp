MSCI has **$85,002 thousand** (about $85.0 million) of operating lease payments due over the next three years (2025 through 2027). That is **about 51.3%** of its $165,605 thousand in total undiscounted operating lease payments. These figures come from the lease maturity table in its 10-K for fiscal 2024, which was the latest 10-K filed as of 16 May 2025.
