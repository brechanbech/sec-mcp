As of December 31, 2024, Shift4's main vendor concentration is that **one third-party vendor handles all of its merchant processing in North America**. The FY2024 10-K does not name the vendor or give a percentage of spend or volume it accounts for.

**What the "Concentration Risk" note says (Note on significant accounting policies, p. 85):**
- "The Company's merchant processing activity in North America is facilitated by one vendor."
- Management believes this vendor "maintains appropriate backup systems and alternative arrangements to avoid a significant disruption of the processing in the event of an unforeseen event."

**More detail from the risk factor "Because we rely on third-party vendors…":**
- **What the vendor does:** In the U.S., EU and Canada, Shift4 depends on a single third-party processor for merchant authorization, processing, risk and chargeback monitoring, accounting, and clearing and settlement. So the dependence extends beyond North America to the EU.
- **Backup:** Shift4 says there is "no assurance" that its backup systems and alternative arrangements would prevent a significant disruption if something unexpected happened at this processor.
- **Renewal risk:** If the agreement ends, or can't be renewed on good terms or at all, the services could be hard to replace. That could hurt operations and profitability.
- **Other vendors:** Shift4 also relies on third parties for parts of its computer systems, software, data centers, telecom networks, and certain software and devices. Some of these suppliers also serve competitors, and Shift4 has no long-term or exclusive contracts with them.
- **Possible consequences of a vendor failure:**
  - lost revenue
  - lost merchants and software partners
  - loss of merchant or cardholder data
  - fines from payment networks
  - regulatory enforcement
  - reputational harm
  - exposure to fraud
  - higher operating costs
  - diversion of management time and resources

**Related dependency (in the same note):** Most of Shift4's revenue comes from processing card transactions. Because Shift4 is not a "member bank" in North America, it relies on a sponsorship agreement with a member bank. If Shift4 breaches that agreement, the bank can terminate it, and Shift4 would then have 180 days to find another sponsor bank.
