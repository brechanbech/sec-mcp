Oracle's effective tax rate for the fiscal year ended 31 May 2024 was **10.9%**, up from 6.8% the year before, an increase of 410bps.

**Effective Tax Rate (FY ended 5/31/2024)**
- FY2024: **10.9%**
- FY2023: **6.8%**
- Delta: **+410bps**

**Income Tax Expense (Provision)**
- FY2024: **$1,274M**
- FY2023: **$623M**
- Change: +$651M, which is about 2.0x the prior year

The tax expense roughly doubled, while the rate rose by about 4.1 points. That means pre-tax income grew much more slowly than the tax charge. The data I pulled didn't include Oracle's pre-tax income for these years, so it isn't shown. The XBRL data doesn't explain why the rate went up; the tax note in the 10-K would.

Later Oracle 10-Ks report the same FY2024 and FY2023 figures. Those filings are dated after 16 May 2025, so I didn't use them.

**Sources**
- Oracle Corp. Form 10-K, filed 2024-06-20, accession 0000950170-24-075605: FY2024 and FY2023 effective tax rate and income tax expense.
- Oracle Corp. Form 10-K, filed 2023-06-20, accession 0000950170-23-028914: confirms the FY2023 figures (6.8% rate, $623M expense).
