I can't split Uber's 2024 revenue growth into take-rate and volume parts with these tools. The split needs Gross Bookings, which these tools couldn't return.

**What I could confirm (from Uber's FY2024 10-K XBRL data):**
- 2024 revenue: $43,978M
- 2023 revenue: $37,281M
- Increase: $6,697M, or about **18.0%**

**Why the split isn't possible:**
- The split depends on Gross Bookings, which stands in for volume. Take rate is revenue divided by Gross Bookings. Uber reports Gross Bookings as a key operating metric in the text of its filings, but doesn't tag it in its XBRL financial data.
- Full-text search shows the phrase "Gross Bookings grew" appears in the FY2024 10-K and the Q4 2024 earnings release. The search returns no text excerpts, though, so I couldn't read the dollar amounts. A check for one specific Gross Bookings phrasing found no match.
- The company-facts tool returned no data at all for Uber, so I couldn't check for a Gross Bookings tag that way either.

**How to finish it:** take the 2024 and 2023 Gross Bookings figures from the MD&A "Key Metrics" section of the 10-K and apply this split:
- Volume effect = Gross Bookings growth × 2023 take rate
- Take-rate effect = change in take rate × 2024 Gross Bookings
- The two add up to the $6,697M revenue increase.

Two cautions for reading the result:
- Some of Uber's revenue changes come from business-model and accounting changes, not pure pricing. Examples are certain Mobility and Freight items that are reported gross versus net.
- Gross Bookings growth can also be shown on a constant-currency basis, which changes the volume figure.

The 10-K discusses both points, so a mechanical take-rate split may overstate how much of the growth really came from "take-rate expansion."
