**About 44.7% of Microsoft's full-time employees were outside the U.S. as of June 30, 2024.**

The FY2024 Form 10-K says that as of June 30, 2024, Microsoft "employed approximately 228,000 people on a full-time basis, 126,000 in the U.S. and 102,000 internationally."

- Total full-time employees: about 228,000
- In the U.S.: about 126,000 (about 55.3%)
- Outside the U.S.: about 102,000
- Calculation: 102,000 ÷ 228,000 ≈ **44.7%**
