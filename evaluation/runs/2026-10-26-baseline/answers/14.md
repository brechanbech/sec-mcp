**US Steel's inventory turnover for FY2024 was about 6.5x**, or roughly 56 days of inventory.

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| FY2024 cost of goods sold (Jan 1 – Dec 31, 2024) | $14,060M |
| Inventory at 31 Dec 2023 | $2,128M |
| Inventory at 31 Dec 2024 | $2,168M |
| Average inventory | ($2,128M + $2,168M) / 2 = $2,148M |
| **Inventory turnover** | $14,060M / $2,148M ≈ **6.55x** |
| Days of inventory | 365 / 6.55 ≈ **55.8 days** |

If you use year-end inventory instead of the average, turnover is $14,060M / $2,168M ≈ 6.49x.

The usual per-company data tools didn't work: the ticker "X" isn't in their list of active tickers. So I took these figures from the SEC's cross-company XBRL data, filtered to US Steel's CIK (1163302).

The $2,168M year-end 2024 inventory figure came from a later US Steel filing (accession 0001163302-25-000030), not from the 10-K. It's probably the Q1 2025 10-Q, but I couldn't confirm what form it is or when it was filed. If that filing is dated after 16 May 2025, the 10-K's balance sheet should show the same 31 Dec 2024 figure, but I didn't check that.

**Sources**
- US Steel Form 10-K for FY2024, filed 2025-01-31, accession 0001163302-25-000018 (FY2024 cost of goods sold; 31 Dec 2023 inventory)
- US Steel filing, accession 0001163302-25-000030 (31 Dec 2024 inventory; form type and filing date not confirmed)
