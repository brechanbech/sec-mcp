MSCI's operating lease payments due in the next three years total about **$85,002 thousand**, which is **about 51.3%** of its $165,605 thousand in total operating lease payments.

These figures come from the latest annual report available as of 16 May 2025, the 10-K for fiscal year 2024 (balance sheet date 31 Dec 2024). They are undiscounted payments ($ thousands):

| Year | Amount |
|---|---|
| 2025 (Year 1) | 30,220 |
| 2026 (Year 2) | 30,361 |
| 2027 (Year 3) | 24,421 |
| **Next 3 years** | **85,002** |
| Total payments, all years | 165,605 |

The 51.3% is 85,002 ÷ 165,605. It is a share of the undiscounted payments, before imputed interest is deducted. The discounted lease liability on the balance sheet would be a smaller number, and I did not pull it.

The tools also returned a later 10-K, filed 6 Feb 2026, but I left it out because it was filed after the 16 May 2025 cutoff.

**Sources**
- MSCI Inc. Form 10-K for FY2024, filed 2025-02-07, accession no. 0001408198-25-000053. These are its XBRL lease payment figures: Year One, Year Two, Year Three and the total.
