## Answer: Not found with these tools

**What's missing:** Netflix's metric is called "average monthly revenue per paying membership." Its 10-Ks for FY2019 through FY2024 all contain that exact phrase; a full-text search found it in each one, including the FY2019 and FY2024 filings. But the search only returns links to the documents, not the text, so I couldn't read the values. Netflix also doesn't report this metric as XBRL data: there are no data under a Netflix-specific taxonomy, and it isn't a standard us-gaap concept. Paid membership counts aren't available that way either, so I couldn't calculate ARPU from revenue and members. I won't give the 2019 or 2024 ARPU from memory.

**Related figure I could verify: total revenue (us-gaap "Revenues")**

| Year | Revenue | Source 10-K |
|---|---|---|
| 2019 | $20,156,447,000 | FY2019 10-K |
| 2020 | $24,996,056,000 | FY2020 10-K |
| 2021 | $29,697,844,000 | FY2021 10-K |
| 2022 | $31,615,550,000 | FY2022 10-K |
| 2023 | $33,723,297,000 | FY2023 10-K |
| 2024 | $39,000,966,000 | FY2024 10-K |

Revenue grew about 93% from 2019 to 2024, roughly 14% a year compounded. That growth combines more paying members with changes in revenue per member, and these numbers can't separate the two. **It is not a proxy for the change in ARPU.**

**To get the actual ARPU figures:** open the "Results of Operations" table in the FY2019 10-K and the FY2024 10-K (links below). Each shows "average monthly revenue per paying membership" next to "paid memberships at end of period."
