**Micron beat its fiscal Q3 2024 GAAP gross margin guidance by about 140 basis points: 26.9% actual against a 25.5% midpoint.**

- **Guidance:** Micron's fiscal Q2 2024 earnings release (filed 2024-03-20) contains both "25.5% ± 1.5%" and "26.5% ± 1.5%". I found these with exact-phrase searches, but the search tool doesn't show the text around a match, so I couldn't read the labels. I've taken 25.5% as the GAAP figure and 26.5% as the non-GAAP one, because non-GAAP margin is normally higher since it excludes items like stock-based compensation. That assignment is an inference, not something I read in the filing.
- **Actual result:** For the quarter ended 30 May 2024, gross profit was $1,832M on revenue of $6,811M, which gives a 26.90% GAAP gross margin.
- **Beat:** 26.90% − 25.5% ≈ +140 bps (+139.8 bps unrounded). That is just within the top of the guided range (24.0%–27.0%).
- **If the labels were the other way round:** If 26.5% were the GAAP midpoint, the beat would be about 40 bps.

Note that the question's "Q3 2024" is Micron's fiscal Q3 2024, which ran from 1 March to 30 May 2024.
