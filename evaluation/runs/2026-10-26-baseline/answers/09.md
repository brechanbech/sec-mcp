**Palantir's 3-year revenue CAGR is about 22.9%, covering FY2021 to FY2024.**

FY2024 is the latest full fiscal year reported as of 16 May 2025.

| Fiscal year (ending Dec 31) | Revenue |
|---|---|
| FY2021 (base year) | $1,541.9M ($1,541,889,000) |
| FY2022 | $1,905.9M |
| FY2023 | $2,225.0M |
| FY2024 (end year) | $2,865.5M ($2,865,507,000) |

**Calculation:** CAGR = (2,865,507,000 ÷ 1,541,889,000)^(1/3) − 1 = (1.8584)^(1/3) − 1 ≈ **22.9%**

Revenue grew about 86% in total over the three years. Growth slowed from 23.6% in 2022 to 16.7% in 2023, then picked up to 28.8% in 2024.

The revenue figures are the XBRL concept "Revenue from Contract with Customer, Excluding Assessed Tax." The data also includes a 10-K filed in February 2026 with FY2025 figures. That is after the 16 May 2025 cutoff, so I left it out.
