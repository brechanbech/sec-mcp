**I estimate BROS gross profit for 2026 at about $467 million.**

**Starting point (FY2024, from the 10-K filed 13 Feb 2025):**
- Revenue: $1,281.0M
- Cost of sales: $940.9M
- Gross profit: $340.1M. Dutch Bros doesn't report a gross profit line, so I calculated it as revenue minus cost of sales.
- Gross margin: 26.55%

**Projection:**
- 2026 revenue = $1,281.0M × 1.30² = $1,281.0M × 1.69 = **$2,164.9M**
- 2026 gross margin = 26.55% − 5.00 pts = **21.55%**
- 2026 gross profit = $2,164.9M × 21.55% = **$466.6M, or about $467M**

**Assumptions:**
- I applied the 30% growth to each of 2025 and 2026, starting from the FY2024 base.
- I read the 500bps compression as a full 5.00-point drop by 2026, not a gradual change over the period.
- Cost of sales comes straight from the company's XBRL "Cost of goods and services sold" figure, which may include costs some analysts would leave out of gross margin.
- The data tool also returned figures from a 10-K filed in February 2026. That's after the 16 May 2025 cutoff, so I didn't use them.

- Dutch Bros Inc. Form 10-K for FY2024, filed 2025-02-13, accession 0001866581-25-000048 (revenue and cost of sales)
