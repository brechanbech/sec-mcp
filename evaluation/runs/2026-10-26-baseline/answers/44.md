In fiscal 2024, Airbnb added back **$1,407 million (about $1.41 billion)** of stock-based compensation expense as a non-cash adjustment in its cash flow statement.

That is up from $1,120 million in 2023 and $930 million in 2022, all figures from the same filing. The data source also returned a 10-K filed 12 Feb 2026. That date is after the 16 May 2025 cutoff, so I left it out, although it shows the same $1,407 million for 2024.

**Sources**
- Airbnb, Inc. Form 10-K for FY2024, filed 2025-02-13, accession no. 0001559720-25-000010 (XBRL concept `ShareBasedCompensation`)
