AMD guided each quarter at a midpoint plus or minus $300 million, so the range was always $600 million wide. As the midpoint went up, that range became a smaller share of it, falling from about 10.5% to about 8.0%.

Q2 2024: $5.4 billion (low) to $6.0 billion (high), 10.5% of midpoint
Q3 2024: $6.4 billion (low) to $7.0 billion (high), 9.0% of midpoint
Q4 2024: $7.2 billion (low) to $7.8 billion (high), 8.0% of midpoint
Q1 2025: $6.8 billion (low) to $7.4 billion (high), 8.5% of midpoint

- **How these were found:** I searched the filings' full text for each exact phrase, "approximately $X.X billion, plus or minus $300 million." Each phrase matched exactly one earnings release, the one published in the quarter before the guided quarter. I didn't open the documents, so the link from each figure to its quarter rests on the filing date of that release.
- **Calculation:** low and high are the midpoint minus and plus $0.3 billion. The percentage is $0.6 billion divided by the midpoint. Unrounded: 10.53%, 8.96%, 8.00% and 8.45%.
