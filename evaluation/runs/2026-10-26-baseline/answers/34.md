Uber's largest adjustment to Adjusted EBITDA for fiscal 2023 was **stock-based compensation expense, about $1.94 billion** ($1,935 million), added back to net income.

The closest other adjustment runs the opposite way. Other income (expense), net was $1.844 billion of income, which is subtracted in the reconciliation. Uber shows it as "(1,844)". So stock-based compensation is the largest adjustment whether you count by absolute size or only count add-backs. Depreciation and amortization was smaller, at $0.823 billion.

I pulled each amount from Uber's tagged financial data, not from the reconciliation table itself. A search confirmed that the FY2023 10-K contains an Adjusted EBITDA reconciliation with a stock-based compensation line, but I didn't read the table. That means I haven't checked every line, such as interest expense or legal and regulatory reserves. The figures above are almost certainly the largest ones, but that part is inferred rather than confirmed line by line.

**Sources**
- Form 10-K for FY2023, filed 2024-02-15, accession 0001543151-24-000012: other income (expense), net of $1,844M; D&A of $823M; the Adjusted EBITDA reconciliation
- Form 10-K for FY2024, filed 2025-02-14, accession 0001543151-25-000008: 2023 stock-based compensation of $1,935M, plus 2023 other income and D&A matching the FY2023 10-K
