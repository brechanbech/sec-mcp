**What the searches show (filings up to 16 May 2025):**
- **Gross revenue retention rate:** This phrase first shows up in the Q3 FY2025 10-Q (filed 2024-12-05). It also appears in the FY2025 10-K (filed 2025-03-11) and the FY2025 annual report to shareholders (filed 2025-04-23). The 10-Qs filed in 2023 and May 2024 don't contain it, so it looks like a new disclosure starting in late 2024.
- **Net revenue retention rate:** This phrase appears only in two letters Workday sent the SEC (filed 2024-09-04 and 2024-09-19), not in any 10-K or 10-Q. Those letters were part of an exchange of SEC staff comments in August–September 2024 that also used "net retention." The timing suggests the gross rate was added to the filings after that exchange. That is my inference; I can't read the letters' text.
- **"Net retention" in 10-Qs:** The phrase also turns up in four 10-Qs filed between May 2023 and May 2024. I couldn't see what it refers to there, so I can't tell whether it was a reported metric or a passing mention.
